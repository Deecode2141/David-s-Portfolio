About

I’m David Salgado, an email designer with experience in e-commerce marketing, focused on creating clear, mobile-friendly emails that are easy to scan and easy to act on. I design promotional, lifecycle, and newsletter emails using strong hierarchy, spacing, and UX principles to guide attention and reduce friction.

At Ardell Beauty, I designed campaign and lifecycle emails while supporting segmentation, A/B testing, deliverability, and automation workflows. By combining design with marketing insight, I create emails that not only look polished, but also support stronger performance across opens, clicks, and engagement.
