Lumera Skin — Promotional Product Email

Audience

Skincare-conscious shoppers looking for hydration-focused, clean beauty products.

Goal

Drive interest and clicks for a featured product through a promotional campaign email.

Design & Mobile Strategy

Mobile-first, single-column layout with strong visual hierarchy. Key benefits and ingredients are surfaced early, with a clear CTA placed above the fold for mobile readers.

Why It Performs

The clean, benefit-led presentation builds trust and makes it easy for users to quickly understand value and take action.
